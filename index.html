Beyblade X battles, edited and shared.

Papi Nerd is a content channel dedicated to Beyblade X. We film top-down matches, edit them into short videos, and publish them on our own social channels. This site documents the in-house tool we use to publish those videos — including to TikTok.

What Papi Nerd does

We record Beyblade X matches with a fixed overhead camera, cut the dead time between launches, and turn each match into a vertical short video. The finished videos are published on the Papi Nerd accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Everything you see published is content we created ourselves and own.

The Papi Nerd publishing tool

To avoid uploading every short by hand on three platforms, we built a small internal tool that takes a finished video file and uploads it to our own channel accounts through each platform's official API. For TikTok it uses the TikTok Content Posting API. The tool only ever posts to the single TikTok account that the channel owner has personally authorized; it does not act on behalf of any other users and is not offered to third parties.

How we use the TikTok API

The tool requests the minimum scopes needed to publish the channel's own videos:

ScopeHow we use it
video.upload Upload a finished video to the channel owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, so it can be reviewed and posted from the TikTok app.
video.publish Directly publish a finished video to the channel owner's own TikTok profile, with the caption and hashtags prepared by the tool. Used only for the owner's own account.
user.info.basic Read the authorized account's basic profile (open id, display name) to confirm the tool is connected to the correct channel account before uploading.

The TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's own computer and are used only to call the Content Posting API. We do not collect, store or share data belonging to any other TikTok users. See our Privacy Policy for details.
